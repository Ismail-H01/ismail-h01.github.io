MACCS+: Matter-enabled curtain and lighting controller

Final year project, BEng Electrical and Electronic Engineering

A self-designed smart curtain and LED lighting controller that joins a smart home through the Matter standard. It is controllable by voice, a web page, physical buttons or a schedule, and the buttons keep working with no network.

  • Custom power PCB with reverse polarity protection, hardware soft start, polyfuses, a TVS diode and a 12 V to 5 V buck stage. The 5 V rail held 5.02 V under full load.
  • Firmware in C and C++ on an ESP32-C6 using ESP-IDF and FreeRTOS, with limit switch interrupts, a stall timeout and saved position across power cycles.
  • Matter control commissioned into Apple Home over Bluetooth LE, including Siri voice commands. Five control paths run at once without conflict.
  • Mechanical design in Fusion 360: a belt-driven, centre-opening mechanism with 3D printed parts, including a sacrificial part that protects the assembly on overrun.
  • Validation: 92 tests, with 83 passed, 7 partial and 2 failed. The failures are reported openly, including Alexa not discovering the device.
  • Debugging: a limit switch feedback loop in the Matter delegate was traced to a command being written from a status callback, and fixed.

02Autumn 2025

Three-phase AC-DC-AC converter and isolated gate drive

EE3PEC power electronics paper, Aston University

A Simulink model of a rectifier and three-phase inverter, paired with a gate drive circuit that was designed, built and tested on the bench. Measured waveforms were compared against the simulation to validate the design.

  • Model: single-phase then three-phase rectifier feeding a voltage-source inverter and a 20 kW balanced load, at a 50 Hz fundamental, modulation index 0.8, 750 Hz switching and 10 µs dead-time.
  • Gate drive PCB with galvanic isolation between the controller logic and the power stage, and matched tracks on both channels to keep signal delay equal.
  • Control: PWM with dead-time generated on a Microsemi SmartFusion board, programmed with Libero and SoftConsole.
  • Results: the gate drive swung between +15 V and −15 V, about 30 V, with dead-time of about 8 µs measured on the oscilloscope.
  • Debugging: a transistor pin-out mismatch between the datasheet and the schematic library was caught during soldering, before power-up.

032024 to 2025

Electronics placement at Renishaw

Industrial placement, Encoder Product Division

A year inside a professional electronics design team, working on PCB design, test planning and qualification testing for precision encoder products. This summary covers what I did at a general level only.

  • Test jig PCB redesigned from scratch in Zuken CR-8000, working from legacy documentation. Obsolete parts were replaced and the board was made to fit the existing jig. It reached the board testing stage before the placement ended.
  • ESD qualification: a self-directed contact discharge sweep up to ±12 kV on a replacement protection device, with a risk assessment completed first. I presented the findings to senior engineers, and the report was approved for production.
  • Protection investigation: structured pulse testing, including at elevated temperature, to assess whether a protection component was needed. The results were reviewed and signed off by stakeholders.
  • Other work: a PCB for a project at another company site, test plan writing, cable builds, and professional soldering training down to 0402 parts.
  • STEM Ambassador: gave an engineering demonstration and careers talk to visiting school groups.
